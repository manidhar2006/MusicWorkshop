\section{My Role}
I built the third stage of our pipeline, \textbf{Music}. I also put the whole pipeline together
as one package and notebook.

\section{What I Did}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Melody.} One note per heartbeat.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Each note starts at the R-peak, lasts 85\% of the R--R interval, and is as loud as
        the ECG at that beat.
      \item The pitch follows the deviation $d_i$ from the mean of the last five intervals. It is
        C4 on time, higher for a faster beat and lower for a slower one.
      \item The melody stays on the C-major pentatonic scale while $|d_i|<12\%$, moves
        chromatically beyond that, and is two octaves away at 50\%.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Bug fixes.} Found and fixed two mapping bugs: faster beats went down instead of
    up, and downward steps left the scale.
  \item \textbf{Arrangement.}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Strings play C--Am--F--G, one chord per four beats. A chord turns diminished
        ($\geq 12\%$) or into a cluster ($\geq 25\%$) as the beats get irregular.
      \item A soft ``lub-dub'' bass-drum pulse follows every beat.
      \item The melody instrument follows the detected emotion.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Rendering.}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item MIDI with \texttt{pretty\_midi}, and audio with FluidSynth and the FluidR3 soundfont,
        normalised to 85\% peak.
      \item A score plot that lines up the ECG, the beat deviations and the notes.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Integration.} The \texttt{ecgmusic} package with its command line
    (\texttt{python -m ecgmusic}), the \texttt{main.ipynb} walkthrough, and the repository
    documentation.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Result}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{A steady heart sounds calm.} All normal pieces stay on the scale, and all 67 of
    their chords are consonant. Three melodies hold C4 as a pedal tone under the moving chords.
  \item \textbf{An irregular heart sounds restless.} AFib pieces leave the scale for 31--66\% of
    their notes, and 68 of their 111 chords turn tense or chaotic.
\end{itemize}
